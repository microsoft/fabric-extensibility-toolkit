Overview

IntuigenceAI Industrial Workers is a Microsoft Fabric workload published by IntuigenceAI. It brings AI-powered Synthetic Engineers directly into your Fabric environment, enabling industrial teams to analyze P&ID diagrams, query plant data in real time, and get expert-level insights — all without leaving Fabric.

AI

AI Synthetic Engineers

Deploy PhD-level AI engineers trained on industrial knowledge directly inside your workspace.

P&ID

P&ID Analysis

Analyze piping & instrumentation diagrams and understand equipment connections automatically.

RT

Real-Time Telemetry

Query live sensor and operational data directly from Eventhouse within your conversations.

GR

Grounded Responses

Every answer is backed by source citations from your own documents and plant data.

Preview Notice: This workload is currently in preview. Features and UI may change. Contact your admin to configure the workload for your organization.

How to Start the Workload

After logging in to Microsoft Fabric, navigate to Workloads in the left sidebar to find the IntuigenceAI workload. Click Learn more to open the workload detail page.

1

Open the Workloads page

In the left sidebar of Microsoft Fabric, click Workloads. Locate the IntuigenceAI (preview) card and click Learn more.

2

Review and open the workload

The IntuigenceAI Industrial Workers detail page shows item types, compatible resources, and the publisher description. Click Open in the "Get Started" panel on the right to begin.

3

Select your workspace

If you are not in the correct workspace, click Workspaces in the left sidebar and choose a workspace with a valid Fabric capacity. The workload is available in all workspaces.

4

Create your first Agent Session

Follow the Get Started guide to create your first Agent Session. You will need to create at least one Data Catalog or Intelligent Board item to proceed.

Admin configuration required: The IntuigenceAI workload must be enabled by your Fabric administrator before it becomes available to end users. If you see the message "Contact your admin to configure this workload", reach out to your IT or Fabric admin.

Item Types

IntuigenceAI Industrial Workers introduces two custom Fabric item types to your workspace:

Data Catalog

A structured repository for all your industrial data assets — documents, P&ID diagrams, equipment specs, and more. The AI workers use the Data Catalog as their grounded knowledge base.

  • Upload and index plant documents
  • Link to Lakehouse data sources
  • Enable source-cited responses from PAM
Intelligent Board

An interactive workspace where your team collaborates with AI workers, runs agent sessions, and visualizes insights derived from plant data and telemetry.

  • Create and manage agent sessions
  • Chat with PAM, your Plant AI Manager
  • Track insights and decisions over time

Creating a Data Catalog

A Data Catalog is the foundation of your IntuigenceAI deployment. It stores and indexes all the industrial knowledge your AI workers will reference when answering questions.

Chatting with PAM — Your Plant AI Manager

PAM (Plant AI Manager) is the primary AI worker you interact with inside an Intelligent Board. PAM has access to your Data Catalog documents and can query real-time telemetry from Eventhouse, allowing it to answer complex operational questions instantly.

What you can ask PAM

You
What is the current status of the heat exchanger on line 3?
PAM
Based on real-time telemetry from Eventhouse (as of 14:32 UTC), the heat exchanger on Line 3 — HX-301 — is operating at 87°C inlet temperature with a differential pressure of 0.4 bar, within normal parameters. [Source: Live telemetry · P&ID HX-301 diagram]
You
Show me the upstream and downstream connections for HX-301.
PAM
HX-301 receives feed from pump P-210 and discharges to separator V-402. The connecting lines are 6"-CS-3021 and 4"-CS-3022 respectively. [Source: P&ID Sheet 12-A, Rev 4]

  • ✦

Analyzing P&ID Diagrams

Piping and Instrumentation Diagrams (P&IDs) are core to any industrial operation. IntuigenceAI workers can parse and understand P&ID content to answer equipment and flow questions without manual interpretation.

How to add P&ID documents

1

Open your Data Catalog

Navigate to the Data Catalog item in your workspace and open it.

2

Upload P&ID files

Upload PDF or image-format P&ID sheets. The AI will automatically index equipment tags, lines, and connections.

3

Ask PAM

Switch to your Intelligent Board and ask PAM questions about equipment connections, instrument tags, or process flows.

Querying Real-Time Telemetry

IntuigenceAI Industrial Workers integrates natively with Microsoft Fabric Eventhouse to bring live operational data into your AI conversations.

Prerequisite: A Fabric Eventhouse must be configured and streaming plant telemetry data before PAM can query it. Work with your Fabric admin to set up the Eventhouse connection.

Once connected, PAM can:

  • Retrieve the current value of any tagged sensor or KPI
  • Identify anomalies and deviations from normal operating ranges
  • Correlate telemetry data with P&ID diagrams and document references
  • Surface real-time context in every grounded response

Grounded Responses & Source Citations

Unlike generic AI tools, every answer PAM gives is grounded — meaning it is directly traceable back to your own industrial data. This is critical for safety, compliance, and operational accuracy.

Each response will include one or more citations in the format:

[Source: P&ID Sheet 07-B, Rev 2 · Live telemetry tag TI-2041]

You can click on citation references within the Intelligent Board to jump directly to the source document or telemetry record.
